# Mortal Anomaly Code of Conduct

Mortal Anomaly is a flat collective where anyone can question anyone. That only
works if questioning stays about the work, never about the person. This code
applies in all Mortal Anomaly spaces: repositories, issues, discussions, chat,
calls, and events, and when representing Mortal Anomaly elsewhere.

## What we expect

- **Question ideas, respect people.** Challenge decisions and work as much as
  you like. Do it with reasons, not insults.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are mistakes, not malice.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Everyone here started with a first pull
  request.
- **Accept being questioned.** Having your work challenged is normal here and is
  not a personal attack.
- **Own your mistakes.** Acknowledge them, fix them, move on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation, public or private.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background,
  ability, or experience level.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or using "anyone can question anyone" as
  cover for harassing a specific person.
- Retaliating against someone for reporting a concern.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the current
**Conduct Stewards** privately at **[CONDUCT EMAIL]**. Current stewards are
listed in the organization's public steward list.

- Reports are kept confidential.
- If a Conduct Steward is involved in the report, they step aside and another
  member takes their place for that case.
- You will get an acknowledgment within **72 hours**.
- Until there are enough members to rotate Conduct Stewards, reports go to the
  founder.

## What happens next

Conduct Stewards review the report, may speak with those involved, and respond
in proportion to what happened:

1. **Private note:** a reminder of this code, for minor issues.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning, with clear expectations.
3. **Temporary ban:** a break from participation for a set period.
4. **Permanent removal:** for serious or repeated violations. Removing a member
   follows the vote described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

Anyone affected by a decision may ask, privately, for it to be reviewed by two
other members not involved in the case.
